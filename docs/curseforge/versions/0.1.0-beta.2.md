<h2>v0.1.0-beta.2</h2>

<p>Adds Patchouli guidebook fixes on top of the 0.1.0-beta.1 translations. No translation changes.</p>

<h3>Added &mdash; Patchouli guidebook fixes</h3>

<p>Overrides three bundled-mod book entries that pointed at recipe IDs that do not exist in the
installed mod versions. In-game this left the recipe widget on those pages blank and spammed
<code>Recipe &lt;id&gt; not found</code> in the client log on every resource reload.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Butchery</strong> &mdash; <code>butchers_guide</code> &rsaquo; <code>blocks/butchers_table</code>:
<code>butchery:crimnsonbutcherstablerecipe</code> &rarr; <code>butchery:crimsonbutcherstablerecipe</code>
(typo in the mod's own book), and <code>jungle:acaciabutcherstablerecipe</code> &rarr;
<code>butchery:junglebutcherstablerecipe</code> (wrong namespace and name).</li>
<li><strong>Butchery</strong> &mdash; <code>butchers_guide</code> &rsaquo; <code>resources/dragon_scale</code>:
the page was declared <code>patchouli:smithing</code> but both recipes
(<code>dragonscaleingotrecipe</code>, <code>dragonscaleblockrecipe</code>) are
<code>minecraft:crafting_shaped</code>. Changed to <code>patchouli:crafting</code>.</li>
<li><strong>Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks</strong> &mdash; <code>iss_guide_book</code> &rsaquo;
<code>progression/late_game</code>: <code>irons_spellbooks:cryomancer_helmet</code> /
<code>pyromancer_helmet</code> &rarr; <code>..._helmet_crafting</code> (the real recipe IDs carry the
<code>_crafting</code> suffix).</li>
</ul>

<p>Apotheosis' <code>apoth_chronicle</code> references <code>minecraft:bookshelf</code>, a valid vanilla
recipe that is craftable in-game &mdash; left untouched; that warning is Patchouli load-order noise,
not a book bug.</p>
